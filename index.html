Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

EasyRecorder respects your privacy. Audio recorded or imported in the app is stored and processed on your device by default. We do not automatically upload your recordings to servers operated by us in order to provide ordinary recording and audio-editing features.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the EasyRecorder iOS app and describes how information is handled when you use recording, playback, audio editing, system-audio capture, and live transcription features. Some features use services provided by Apple.

2. Information processed on your device

To provide features you choose to use, EasyRecorder may process the following on your device:

  • Audio files you record, capture or import;
  • Recording names, durations, formats, categories, favorite status and related metadata;
  • Waveform data, trimmed or merged audio, and temporary files;
  • App preferences and recording or transcription settings;
  • Recognition results generated when you choose to use live transcription.

These files and data are primarily stored locally in the app. We do not sell your recordings or transcription content for marketing purposes.

3. Microphone, system-audio capture and speech recognition

Microphone: The app requests microphone access when you use features that require it, such as microphone recording. You can revoke this access in iOS Settings.

System-audio capture: When you actively start system broadcasting or system-audio capture, EasyRecorder uses Apple-provided interfaces, including ReplayKit, to receive audio that iOS permits it to capture. Availability and behavior are subject to iOS permissions and restrictions.

Live transcription: Speech transcription may be performed on-device where supported by the operating system and configuration. When Apple's speech recognition services are used, audio may be processed by Apple, depending on your iOS version, language and available capabilities. Such processing is governed by Apple's privacy practices. You can choose not to use live transcription and manage speech recognition permissions in Settings.

4. Local files, sharing and backups

Finished recordings are stored on your device by default. You may be able to access, copy, move, export or share them through the iOS Files app. When you choose to share a file with another service or recipient, that recipient processes it under their own terms and privacy practices.

If iOS or iCloud device backups are enabled, Apple may back up some app files according to your device backup settings. This is separate from the app uploading recordings to servers operated by us. We encourage you to keep appropriate backups of important recordings.

5. Apple services and third-party components

The app uses Apple-provided audio recording, ReplayKit and Speech interfaces. Certain system functions may involve Apple processing the information needed to deliver those functions.

As of the effective date of this policy, EasyRecorder does not integrate advertising SDKs for cross-app tracking and does not provide recording contents to third-party advertisers. If new services materially change how information is processed, we will update this policy and provide any required notice or consent.

6. In-app purchases

If you use in-app purchases offered by EasyRecorder, Apple App Store processes payments. We do not directly receive or store your payment card number or payment password. The app may use purchase eligibility and transaction status provided by Apple to unlock or restore purchased functionality.

7. Retention and deletion

You can manage and delete recordings in the app. Content moved to the Trash may remain on your device until you permanently delete it. After permanent deletion, the app attempts to remove the corresponding local files and records.

Uninstalling the app generally removes data in its local app container, but copies you previously exported, shared or stored in Files, or copies maintained in system backups, may not be removed at the same time. You may need to manage those copies separately.

8. Your choices

You can control microphone and speech recognition permissions in iOS Settings. You can choose whether to use system-audio capture, live transcription or system sharing features, and manage locally stored recordings using the available app controls.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to features, technical implementations or applicable requirements. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. For material privacy changes, we will provide further notice as required by applicable law.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions about EasyRecorder, email us at ifimance@gmail.com .
